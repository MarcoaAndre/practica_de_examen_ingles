⚡ Simple Past Tense
Acciones completadas en el pasado
Forma Estructura Ejemplo
Affirmative Subject + verb(past) + object The earthquake shook the city.
Negative Subject + did not + verb(base) The flood did not destroy the town.
Yes/No Question Did + subject + verb(base)? Did the volcano erupt last night?
WH-Question WH-word + did + subject + verb(base)? Where did the tsunami hit?
💡 Truco clave En negativas y preguntas con DID → el verbo va en BASE FORM (sin -ed ni irregular).
✅ Did the flood destroy...?    ❌ Did the flood destroyed...?
🔄 Past Progressive
Acción en progreso en un momento del pasado
Sujeto Verbo "to be" pasado Verbo + ing
I / He / She / It was cleaning the beach.
You / We / They were cleaning the beach.
💡 Cuándo usarlo Se usa para acciones que estaban en progreso cuando otra ocurrió.
"She was studying when the earthquake hit."

✍️ Párrafo Persuasivo (W.2)
Sobre problemas ambientales y ayudar a la naturaleza

Un buen párrafo persuasivo tiene:

1.
Topic sentence — Afirma tu posición claramente.
Humans are destroying the environment through pollution and deforestation.
2.
Supporting details — Da razones/ejemplos usando el vocabulario.
Last year, a forest fire threatened many animal species...
3.
Concluding sentence — Refuerza tu opinión.
We must protect and save our planet before it is too late.
